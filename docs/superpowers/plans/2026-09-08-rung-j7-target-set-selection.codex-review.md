## CRITICAL

- **Capped AI choices are still rejected by list membership.** `aiHandlers` checks the worker’s command against `legalCommands` before reaching `narrateApply`; a valid `chooseTargets` or activation omitted by the cap is discarded and may be selected repeatedly on the deterministic retry. Location: [useGame.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/apps/web/src/game/useGame.ts:475). Suggested change: replace the membership check with `isLegal(forState, command)`, include its refusal reason in the warning, and remove the now-unused `legal` parameter from `narrateApply`.

- **`isLegal` rejects valid capped activations.** After `activationCheck` succeeds, it requires the exact target set and payment to appear in `legalCommands`; target sets after `all.slice(0, 64)` therefore fail even though `applyActivateAbility` accepts them. It also rejects legal non-minimal activation payments. Location: [legal.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/legal.ts:113), [activate.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/activate.ts:279). Suggested change: validate activation payments directly with the CP requirement and `generateCp`/`canPay`; never consult an enumerated target list from `isLegal`.

## HIGH

- **The activation cap happens after the combinatorial explosion.** `activationTargetSets` eagerly builds every subset, then `activationsWithMeta` slices the completed array. The AI also iterates the full array without any cap. Location: [activate.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/activate.ts:177), [legal.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/legal.ts:301), [candidates.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/ai/src/candidates.ts:318). Suggested change: expose lazy/bounded activation-target generation and give AI activations a ranked bounded policy analogous to `chooseTargetsCandidates`.

- **The generic `isLegal` fallback is not equivalent to `apply` and is expensive.** A cast with a valid overpayment or reordered payment is accepted by `apply` but rejected because it is absent from the minimal, canonically ordered list. Each cast validation also re-enumerates payments for every cast and activation in the position. Location: [legal.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/legal.ts:121), [cast.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/cast.ts:115), [cp.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/cp.ts:137). Suggested change: add direct predicates for casts and remaining command variants; validate payment sources and coverage without enumeration.

## MEDIUM

- **The “64-set cap” can emit more than 64 sets.** `boundedSubsets` fills all 64 slots and then appends every `largest()` set. The acceptance test explicitly weakens the requirement to `DEFAULT_SET_CAP + 1`; attacks may exceed it by several element parties. Location: [legal.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/legal.ts:171), [legal-cap.test.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/test/legal-cap.test.ts:39). Suggested change: reserve slots for deduplicated largest sets before filling the sample and assert `sets.length <= DEFAULT_SET_CAP`.

- **The cap is computed from raw combinations, not legal sets.** Attack enumeration can report `capped: true` while returning the complete legal list, and can still scan a large number of illegal mixed-element subsets. Location: [legal.ts](/Users/danielroach/repos/fftcg-game/.claude/worktrees/rung-a-heuristic-ai/packages/engine/src/legal.ts:157). Suggested change: generate attack parties by element groups with cross-group deduplication, stopping after `cap + 1` legal results.
